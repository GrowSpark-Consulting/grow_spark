FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this just a branding service?

No. Branding is one component of the engagement. We examine positioning, offer, pricing, customer experience, digital experience and sales as part of the broader transformation.

Can you help us increase our prices?

Where the underlying value and market position support it, we can help redesign positioning, offers and customer experience to strengthen pricing power. Pricing recommendations should always be based on customer research, competitive context and business economics.

Do you redesign websites?

Digital experience can form part of the transformation programme. Where technology implementation is required, the appropriate specialist delivery team can be involved.

Can an existing established brand become premium?

Yes. A brand does not necessarily need to be rebuilt from scratch. In many cases, the opportunity is to reposition and elevate what already exists.
